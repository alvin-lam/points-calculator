Privacy

Airline Points Calculator

What this tool stores

Everything you enter stays in this browser on this device. It is not sent to us or anyone else.

The calculator saves these settings in your browser's local storage:

  • Your light, dark or automatic mode.
  • The last airline program and status you chose.
  • When you closed the "Add to Home Screen" message.

Fares, fees, airports and card choices are never saved. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it.

What your device tells the tool

The calculator reads your device's language and region to format numbers, money and dates, and to choose miles or kilometres. This stays on your device.

Services we use

Everything the page needs, including its fonts and airport list, loads from this site. Your browser does not contact any other service while you use the calculator. Links to airline websites open only when you choose them.

The calculator has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
